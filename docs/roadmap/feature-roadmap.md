## 🗺️ Phased Rollout Plan

### Phase 1: Quick Wins & High Engagement
- 🎙️ **Telegram Voice Notes**: Speak voice memos on the go to log tasks, expenses, and reminders.
- 🌅 **Executive Briefings**: Daily 7:30 AM summary of weather, calendar meetings, and priority tasks.
- 🧾 **Receipt Image Drop**: OCR extraction directly mapping line items to expenses.
- ⚡ **Command Palette**: Fast keyboard-driven modal in Next.js.

### Phase 2: Core Growth & Ecosystem
- 📅 **Google Calendar Sync**: Real-time event scheduling and conflict alerts.
- 📊 **Generative UI**: Render live interactive Recharts graphs directly within chat stream responses.
- ⏰ **Natural Language Cron Engine**: Proactive condition-based alerts ("*Ping me if spending exceeds $500*").

### Phase 3: Cognitive Depth & Multi-Agent Swarms
- 📚 **Second Brain Vector RAG**: Hybrid search over personal documents, PDFs, and notes in `pgvector`.
- 🕵️ **Deep Research Sub-Agent**: Recursive multi-source web investigations producing structured reports.
- ✉️ **Email Triage**: Inbox summarization and smart drafts backed by HITL safety gates.
